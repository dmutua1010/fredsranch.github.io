Overview Why this structure improves communication and execution

Executive clarity

The Chairman directs the General Manager directly, eliminating unnecessary bureaucratic layers that slowed product, menu, and operations decisions.

This gives Fred’s Ranch a direct operating pipeline: strategy → execution → daily operations.

Governance + compliance

Legal authority is formalized through board-issued directives, while SOPs and IP controls ensure kitchen innovations and brand-protected recipes are managed with clear accountability.

Faster decisions

Menu changes can move from board idea to operational rollout in hours rather than days or weeks.

Better controls

Financial, operational, and legal signals are connected to decision owners and not buried within a dense hierarchy.

Resilience for growth

New roles in food safety, supply planning, and direct-to-customer growth create the platform needed for expansion.
